## Brand & Style
The design system manifests an uncompromising collision between mid-century Eastern European municipal bureaucracy and haunting expressionist oil portraiture. It frames personal financial discipline not as sterile arithmetic, but as a psychological autopsy—an investigative docket where every debt is a compromised moral compromise, and every savings pool is a quiet precinct reserve.

### Core Principles
- **Bureaucracy as Artifact:** Ledgers, ledger lines, official stamped case files, carbon-copied tabs, and municipal field reports replace contemporary fintech slickness.
- **Expressionist Atmospheric Weight:** Deep ash shadows, smudged oil pigment textures, and oxidized maritime hues create an introspective, literary tension without muddying transactional clarity.
- **Narrative Auditing:** Spending histories behave like recursive interior dialogues, interrogating impulsive splurges as cognitive failures and discipline as hard-won mental fortitude.

### Design Movement Synthesis
A dark tactile fusion of **Bureaucratic Brutalism** and **Painterly Skeuomorphism**. Dense, structural layouts anchored by precise hairline rules, ledger tab markers, distressed official insignias, and aged parchment surfaces resting in charcoal chiaroscuro.

## Colors
The palette evokes wet cobblestones, cheap tobacco paper, rusted coastal cranes, and faded revolutionary manifestos. The system operates on a dark, oil-stained foundation with targeted parchment layers for legible ledger sheets.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#D96628` - Revolutionary Amber):** The burning impulse. Applied to primary call-to-actions, urgent over-budget warnings, transaction flags, active investigation pins, and visceral psychological stats (Physique / Volition alarms).
- **Secondary (`#2E4A44` - Oxidized Sea Green / Revachol Teal):** The cold civic waters. Used for positive financial inflows, balanced accounts, analytical metrics (Intellect / Logic), and foundational panel boundaries.
- **Tertiary (`#D5CAA8` - Aged Tobacco Parchment):** The official archival plane. Serves as high-contrast foreground typography on dark surfaces, specimen label backdrops, and active balance ledger panels.
- **Neutral (`#191B1D` - Cigarette Ash Charcoal):** The gutter and canvas floor. Provides the deep, low-luminance substrate where numbers and case notes linger.

### Functional Modifiers
- **Parchment Pale (`#E6DECA`):** Emphasized balances, primary numerical displays, and docket receipts.
- **Oxidized Slag (`#1B3530`):** Depressed containers, table headers, and dormant status cards.
- **Dried Blood / Deficit (`#B84A1C`):** Overdrawn markers, penalty fees, and recurring liabilities.
- **Void Floor (`#111315`):** Root canvas background and deep shadow wells.

## Typography
Typographic execution relies entirely on the austere authority of monospaced typography, lending every ledger entry, headline, and narrative body the precise feel of a municipal typewriter or teletype record.

### Font Roles
- **Space Mono (Display, Headlines, Narrative Body, Labels, Figures, Docket IDs, Timestamps):** Represents the Revacholian bureaucratic apparatus. It handles all numerical currencies, percentage trackers, ledger hashes, case file numbers, and categorizations with rigid tabular alignment.

### Numerical Handling
All currency values must use `Space Mono` with tabular numbers (`tnum`) enabled to ensure vertical alignment down accounting columns. Ledger transaction signs (`+` / `-`) sit flush to left bounds, while values align hard right.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout simulates physical municipal dossier folders, field notebooks, and ledger tables clipped together. It follows an uncompromising vertical rhythm calibrated to single-hand mobile operation.

### Mobile Grid Philosophy
- **Column Mechanics:** 4-column layout on mobile, expanding to an 8-column layout on wide viewport instances. 
- **Ledger Rhythms:** Gaps mirror the precise horizontal rules of printed accounting registers. Dense data presentation is embraced rather than diluted with excessive whitespace, reinforcing the institutional, ink-heavy atmosphere.
- **Tactile Margins:** Canvas margins mirror paper borders (`1rem` on phones), allowing elements to anchor against rigid outer frame lines.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation eschews modern diffuse drop shadows and clean blurs in favor of stacked physical layers: ink washes, paper overlays, stamped borders, and heavy carbon borders.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Base Surface (Ground Charcoal):** `#111315` textured with subtle canvas tooth.
2. **Container Tier (Oxidized Slag / Ash Paper):** `#191B1D` bordered with `#2E4A44` at 1px hairline weight.
3. **Parchment Inset (The Dossier Surface):** `#D5CAA8` flat fills with `#191B1D` typography for maximum legibility inside itemized receipts and active audit screens.
4. **Physical Drop Shadows:** Hard-edged, offset block shadows (e.g., `2px 2px 0px #0A0B0C`, no blur). Components evoke physical cardstock placed beneath a desk lamp.
5. **Hairline Etching:** Dividers are 1px borders tinted in `#2E4A44` (alpha 40%) or `#D5CAA8` (alpha 20%) to mimic copperplate engraving lines.

## Shapes
Forms feature soft, rounded corners that balance municipal institutional forms with approachable, tactile physical cardstock.

### Shape Language
- **Rounded Radii (`roundedness: 2`):** Containers, cards, modals, and buttons feature softened, friendly corners (0.5rem base roundedness, 1rem for `rounded-lg`, and 1.5rem for `rounded-xl`), recalling handled index cards and stamped government manifests with worn edges.
- **Chamfered Cuts:** Specific specialized UI elements—notably Thought Cabinet entries and specimen ledger headers—utilize 45-degree corner notches (clip-paths) of `8px` or `12px` to evoke hexagonal badge iconography.
- **Badge Silhouettes:** Tags and fiscal statuses adopt ribbon-cut edges or clipped hexagonal silhouettes rather than sharp pills.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary (Revolutionary Directive):** Soft-rounded block in `#D96628` with `#111315` mono text (`label-lg`), featuring a 1px solid black inner hairline border and hard `2px 2px 0px #000000` offset shadow.
- **Secondary (Bureaucratic Endorsement):** Dark slate `#1B3530` background, `#D5CAA8` mono text, framed in a 1px `#2E4A44` oxidized teal border.
- **Destructive / Overdraft Protocol:** Inverted `#B84A1C` with scorched-ash borders and typewriter warning glyphs.

### Dialogue-Tree Ledger Entries
- Transactions render as nested dialogue branches. The merchant/asset name sits as an interlocutor cue in `Space Mono` (`headline-sm`), followed by a small, monospaced timestamp and category indicator.
- Spending/investment entries expand downwards into dialogue options (e.g., `"[LOGIC: SUCCESS] A calculated allocation below target weight. Equilibrium approaches."` vs `"[INLAND EMPIRE: WARNING] Over-concentrated in volatile specimens."`).
- Line items connect vertically using a 1px dotted teal branch line on the left margin.

### Thought Cabinet Hexagon Specimen Cards
- Financial saving goals, investment holdings, and portfolios manifest as "Thoughts" incubating within the internal mental apparatus.
- Cards feature chamfered hexagon corners, an oxidized copper-green border, a mono progress ticker (`"ALLOCATION: 74% OF TARGET"`), and high-contrast monochrome badges illustrating the asset class.

### Psychological Stat Bars (Asset Class Allocation Mapping)
- The four asset classes in the MVP align with the four psychological stat bars:
  - **Intellect (Teal `#2E4A44`):** **Renda Fixa** (CDI, IPCA+, Pré-fixado — the logical, foundational precinct reserve).
  - **Psyche (Parchment `#D5CAA8`):** **FIIs (Fundos Imobiliários)** (brick-and-mortar real estate yields & patrimonial stability).
  - **Physique (Amber `#D96628`):** **Ações** (productive industrial force, equity shares, B3 corporations).
  - **Motorics (Gold/Ochre `#C49A45`):** **Criptomoedas** (high-velocity, decentralized digital reflex).
- Rendered as segment-stepped horizontal progress blocks with soft dividers and monospaced fraction/percentage labels (`[||||||....] 60% / META 50%`).

### Cards & Dossiers
- Bound by heavy 1px carbon borders with corner registration crosshairs (`+`) rendered in typewriter mono.
- Headers are stamped with uppercase case-docket metadata (`"DOCKET REF: CART-01 // PATRIMÔNIO"`).
- Backgrounds employ a dark charcoal tint `#191B1D` with subtle horizontal rules reminiscent of lined fiscal record books.

### Input Fields
- Underlined ledger entry style. No enclosed bubble inputs: an open `#D5CAA8` parchment baseline rule with a blinking block cursor. Monospaced placeholder text in muted amber-grey (`#D5CAA8` at 40% opacity). Numerical amounts display oversized with trailing currency codes (`R$` / `BRL`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Soft-rounded 14x14px indicators with an `X` stamped in `#D96628` for checkboxes.
- Radios use rotated square diamond indicators (`◆`) rather than circular discs.
